# aiMUSIC design plan

Reading this as: a B2B product landing for owners of cafes, restaurants, salons, hotels, fitness studios, and retail spaces, using the existing aiNOW landing family with a music-specific interactive proof.

## Dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 6. The family shell stays stable while the product sections use offset layouts and changing visual anchors.
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 6. Motion explains generation, playback, and daypart changes. Reduced motion keeps every final state visible.
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 4. The page remains airy and sales-oriented while the music controls stay legible.

## Direction

- Theme: pristine light with the existing family dark-mode tokens.
- Hero architecture: family asymmetric split.
- Section system: alternating product controls, daypart timeline, queue, and contract panel.
- Accent: coral rose `#D33F67`, backed by soft pink and deep berry tokens.
- Typography: the family Geist and FiraGO stack.
- Motion grammar: waveform playback plus short state transitions driven by user actions.
- Material: white paper surfaces, tinted borders, and restrained depth.

## Signature element

The sound fingerprint is generated from the selected venue profile and repeated across the hero, daily sequence, and track register. The hero play button produces a short browser-generated sound sketch so the interface is functional without claiming that the sample is a finished customer soundtrack.

## Page narrative

1. Describe the venue.
2. Set the character of the sound.
3. Shape the day by time period.
4. Review a playlist made for one location.
5. Fix the usage scope in writing before launch.
